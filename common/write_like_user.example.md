Copy this file to `common/write_like_user.md` and replace the placeholder guidance
with rules that reflect your actual voice and writing preferences.

copy each time it detects meaningful patterns in your edits.

## Overview

This file tells the LLM how to write in your voice. The more specific these rules
are, the less editing you'll need to do on rough drafts.

Describe how you actually write: sentence length, what phrases you avoid, what
tone you prefer, how you open paragraphs. Draw from emails, previous cover letters,
or anything else you've written that felt authentic.

## Sentence and Paragraph Structure

- [Describe your preferred sentence length — short and direct? or more composed?]
- [Do you use semicolons? Em-dashes? Bullet-like parallel structure?]
- [How do you open a paragraph — with a topic sentence, a fact, a question?]

Example placeholder rules:
- Prefer short, declarative sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Avoid compound sentences joined by "and" when a period would be cleaner.
- Open paragraphs with the most important fact, not a windup.

## Tone

- [Formal or conversational? Confident or measured?]
- [How do you handle self-promotion — direct, understated, factual?]
- [What's your relationship to enthusiasm — genuine or reserved?]

Example placeholder rules:
- Confident but not boastful. State accomplishments plainly.
- Avoid corporate filler: "leverage," "synergies," "passionate professional," "results-driven."
- Don't thank the reader in the opening line.

## Cover Letter Specifics

- [Do you prefer a story opening or a direct statement of intent?]

Example:
